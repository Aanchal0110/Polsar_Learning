  • Copernicus Browser

    The Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem Browser serves as a central hub for accessing, exploring and utilizing the wealth of Earth observation and environmental data provided by the Copernicus Sentinel constellations, contributing missions. Based on Sentinel Hub's EO Browser, users can visualise, compare and analyse and download all this data for a variety of applications, from environmental monitoring and disaster management to urban planning and agriculture.

  • NASA Earthdata Search

    NASA's Earthdata Search is a comprehensive platform for accessing Earth science datasets, offering an extensive collection of satellite imagery and analysis-ready products. It supports studies of the biosphere, cryosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere, with data sourced from NASA's Distributed Active Archive Centers (DAACs). Beyond standard datasets, it provides specialized land cover data, such as permafrost maps and wetland types, enabling in-depth environmental analysis. Designed for researchers, educators, and policymakers, Earthdata Search simplifies data discovery and promotes a deeper understanding of global processes, making it an indispensable resource for addressing climate change, ecosystem monitoring, and other critical Earth science challenges.

  • Alaska Satellite Facility

    The Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) is a data processing facility and satellite-tracking ground station within the Geophysical Institute at the University of Alaska Fairbanks. Its mission is to make remote-sensing data accessible. Its work is central to polar processes research, including wetlands, glaciers, sea ice, climate change, permafrost, flooding, and land cover, such as changes in the Amazon rainforest.

  • Bhuvan Indian Geo-Platform of ISRO

    Bhuvan is an Indian web-based utility which allows users to explore a set of geographic content prepared by the Indian Space Research Organisation. The content which the utility serves is mostly restricted to within Indian boundaries and is offered in four regional languages. The content includes thematic maps related to disasters, agriculture, water resources, land cover, and processed satellite data generated by ISRO.
