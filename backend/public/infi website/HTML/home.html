INTRODUCTION TO REMOTE SENSING

Remote sensing is the science of acquiring information about objects or areas from a distance, typically from aircraft or satellites. This technology involves the use of various types of sensors that detect and measure electromagnetic radiation reflected or emitted from the Earth's surface.

INTRODUCTION TO MICROWAVE REMOTE SENSING

Microwave remote sensing is a subset of remote sensing that involves the use of microwave radiation (with wavelengths ranging from 1 millimeter to 1 meter) to gather information about the Earth's surface and atmosphere. This type of remote sensing can penetrate clouds, rain, and vegetation.
