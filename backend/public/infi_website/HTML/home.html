INTRODUCTION TO REMOTE SENSING

Remote sensing is the science of acquiring information about objects or areas from a distance, typically from aircraft or satellites. This technology involves the use of various types of sensors that detect and measure electromagnetic radiation reflected or emitted from the Earth's surface.

INTRODUCTION TO MICROWAVE REMOTE SENSING

Microwave remote sensing is a subset of remote sensing that involves the use of microwave radiation (with wavelengths ranging from 1 millimeter to 1 meter) to gather information about the Earth's surface and atmosphere. This type of remote sensing can penetrate clouds, rain, and vegetation.

INTRODUCTION TO OPTICAL REMOTE SENSING

Optical remote sensing uses visible, infrared, and thermal radiation to capture images of the Earth's surface. Optical remote sensing is a technique that uses sensors on satellites, aircraft, or drones to detect and record natural radiation (sunlight or thermal emission) reflected or emitted from the Earth’s surface in different parts of the electromagnetic spectrum, mainly visible, near-infrared (NIR), shortwave infrared (SWIR), and thermal infrared (TIR) regions.

About the Platform

PolSARLearn is a dedicated knowledge and capacity-building platform for Microwave Remote Sensing, with a special focus on Polarimetric Synthetic Aperture Radar (PolSAR). The website aims to make PolSAR concepts accessible to students, researchers, educators, and professionals by providing structured learning resources ranging from fundamental principles to advanced topics. PolSARLearn serves as a comprehensive hub that brings together information on leading experts in the field, national and international higher education institutions, paid and open-source software, satellite data download sources, and carefully curated learning resources, including books, research papers, and video lectures. By consolidating these resources in one place, the platform supports self-learning, academic research, skill development, and collaboration within the microwave remote sensing community. Our mission is to promote awareness, enhance technical competence, and foster research and innovation in microwave remote sensing by providing reliable, up-to-date, and easily accessible educational resources for learners worldwide.
